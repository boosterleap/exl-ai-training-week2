# POL-COMM-030 Commercial Property Coverage

Policy type: commercial_property
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Covered causes of loss

The policy covers direct physical loss to the insured commercial building and its
contents from fire, windstorm, vandalism, and equipment breakdown, up to the
building limit shown on the declarations page, after the USD 2,500 deductible.

## Business income

If a covered loss suspends operations, the policy reimburses lost business income
and continuing expenses for up to 12 months, subject to a 72-hour waiting period.

## Documentation

Claims must include a contractor repair estimate, photos of the affected area, and
a statement of loss within 30 days of the reported date of loss.
