# POL-COMM-030 Commercial Property Exclusions

Policy type: commercial_property
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Equipment malfunction limitation

Damage caused by a covered equipment breakdown is included, but the resulting
mechanical part itself is excluded; only consequential damage to the building and
other contents is payable.

## Flood and earth movement exclusion

Flood, surface water, and earth movement are excluded. Coverage requires a
separate commercial flood endorsement, which is not included on this policy.

## Wear, tear, and neglect

Gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, and lack of maintenance are excluded
regardless of cause of loss.
