# RAIL-SLA-002 Intermodal Rail Transit Times

Mode: rail
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Contracted transit windows

Standard intermodal rail corridors within the continental US are contracted at
3-5 calendar days terminal-to-terminal, plus up to 24 hours for drayage at
each end.

## Interchange scans

A terminal scan is expected at every interchange point at least once every
72 hours. A gap beyond 72 hours with no scan should be treated as a
missing-scan exception, not assumed to be routine transit.

## Customer notification requirement

Any shipment with a missing-scan exception must be escalated to the control
tower within 4 hours of the gap being detected, not held for the customer to
ask first.
