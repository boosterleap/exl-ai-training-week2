# CARGO-CLAIM-010 Cargo Damage Claims

Applies to: ocean, rail
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Filing window

A cargo damage claim must be filed within 7 days of the damage being discovered,
with photos of the damage, the packing list, and a surveyor report where the
claim value exceeds USD 5,000.

## Liability cap

Carrier liability for cargo damage is capped per the applicable rate tariff and
container weight, not the full replacement value of the goods, unless the
shipper declared a higher value and paid the associated premium in advance.

## Fraud review trigger

Any damage claim flagged with a suspicion of reused or duplicated photographic
evidence must be routed to special_investigations before any settlement offer
is communicated to the customer.
