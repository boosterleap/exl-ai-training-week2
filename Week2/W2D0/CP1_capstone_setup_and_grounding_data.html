Capstone Phase 1 — Setup & Grounding Data

Thirteen topics in, you've used Claude Code to build, test, and guard small pieces of software. Now you direct it to build a six-phase capstone that is a microcosm of the whole week: a Bank Loan Approval Assistant that resolves inbound applicant emails the same way the insurance/logistics threads do all week — look up the real record, cite the real policy, never invent a term. Phase 1 builds the ground truth every later phase depends on.

Concept: Why this capstone, and why it starts with data

The shape of the assistant

CLAUDE.md's ground rules for this whole course apply here too: never invent a policy term or a decision status; every reply must cite a specific record and a specific document; approving, denying, or overriding a loan decision requires an explicit human approval step (built in Phase 5); prefer a small number of clearly-named tools over one do-everything tool.

What Phase 1 produces

  • loans.db (SQLite: applicants, loans) seeded from a real public dataset
  • 4 underwriting policy documents — one per loan product, plus one product with no document at all, on purpose
  • inbound_emails.csv — 12 synthetic customer emails referencing real loan IDs from the database
  • A data README documenting the schema, the product→policy mapping, and the deliberate gap

Real data, not a toy CSV

Rather than hand-inventing applicant records, this phase fetches the real, public Analytics Vidhya Loan Prediction dataset — 491 unique applicants with genuine messiness (missing credit history, missing loan amounts, missing self-employment status on dozens of rows). That messiness is the point: a production assistant has to handle incomplete records gracefully, not just clean textbook ones.

The deliberate grounding gap

Exactly like the insurance thread's commercial_package product (no policy doc) and the logistics thread's air mode (no SLA doc), this capstone gives one loan product — gold_loan — no underwriting policy document at all. Three of the twelve "live" cases in inbound_emails.csv are gold_loan cases. The assistant must recognize it has no grounding for that product and escalate rather than fabricate an eligibility rule — that behavior is exercised for real starting in Phase 2.

Key Learning Objectives

By the end of Phase 1, you will:

  • Pull a real public dataset over the network and turn it into a queryable SQLite database
  • Design a two-table schema (applicants/loans) that separates stable demographic facts from case-state facts
  • Write short, specific policy documents with numeric, citable terms (ratios, SLAs, surcharges) — not vague prose
  • Understand why a deliberate grounding gap is a teaching tool, not an oversight
  • Produce a data contract (a README) that every later phase can trust without re-deriving it

Debrief A real underwriting system never sees only the open cases — it needs the historical book (rates of approval, typical income ranges, prior status) as context for scoring new and in-flight applications, and a tool call resolving Loan_ID shouldn't have to know in advance whether that ID is "live" or historical. 3Write the policy documents — with a deliberate gap
Claude Code chat panel
Prompt
Now write 4 short underwriting policy documents as Markdown files in
W2D0/capstone/data/policies/, one per loan product except gold_loan (which
gets NO document — that's deliberate, don't create one):

- LOAN-HOME-010_underwriting.md (home_loan): Credit_History must be 1 to
  auto-process; combined monthly obligations (LoanAmount*1000/Loan_Amount_Term)
  must not exceed 40% of combined monthly income; Rural properties get a 0.25pt
  rate surcharge (not an eligibility change); document checklist; 10-business-day SLA.
- LOAN-PERSONAL-020_underwriting.md (personal_loan): Credit_History 0 is a firm
  decline (no manual-review path, since it's unsecured); 35% income ratio cap;
  3+ Dependents get a 0.15pt surcharge; 3-business-day SLA.
- LOAN-BUSINESS-030_underwriting.md (business_loan): Self_Employed=Yes applicants
  need Credit_History=1 AND combined income >= 4000 for auto-processing, else
  manual review; 40% income ratio cap; a rejected applicant may request ONE
  re-review within 30 days with new income documentation, but the re-review
  itself needs human sign-off; document checklist including 2 years of tax
  filings for self-employed applicants; 7-business-day SLA.
- LOAN-EDU-040_underwriting.md (education_loan): Credit_History 0/missing is
  still eligible for auto-processing IF a co-applicant is present
  (CoapplicantIncome > 0), otherwise manual review; 45% income ratio cap (most
  permissive of the four); document checklist including enrollment
  confirmation; 5-business-day SLA.

Keep each document short and concrete -- specific numbers a reply can cite,
not vague language like "generally" or "in most cases."

Have Claude Code explain, in its own words, why gold_loan getting no document matters — you want to hear it describe this as "the assistant should refuse/escalate here," not shrug past it.
