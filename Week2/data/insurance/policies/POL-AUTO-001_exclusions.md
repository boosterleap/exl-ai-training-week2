# POL-AUTO-001 Exclusions and Limits

Policy type: comprehensive auto
Related policy: POL-AUTO-001

## General exclusions

The policy does not cover intentional damage, racing, or commercial ride-share use
without a commercial rider.

## Rental car limits

Rental reimbursement does not apply when the vehicle is available for repair within
72 hours, or when the driver lives outside the rated garaging state without an
out-of-state rider on file.

## Theft reporting

Theft claims require a police report number. Delay beyond 24 hours may reduce rental
reimbursement to 15 days.
