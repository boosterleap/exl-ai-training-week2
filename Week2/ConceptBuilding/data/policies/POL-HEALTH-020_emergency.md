# POL-HEALTH-020 Emergency Services

Policy type: group health
Related policy: POL-HEALTH-020

## Emergency definition

Emergency services are covered when a prudent layperson would believe delay would
seriously jeopardize health, including severe chest pain and major trauma.

## Post-stabilization

After stabilization, non-emergency services at out-of-network facilities may require
transfer or prior auth for continued inpatient days beyond 48 hours.
