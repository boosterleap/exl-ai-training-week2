# CARGO-CLAIM-010 Missing Shipment Handling

Applies to: ocean, rail
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Definition

A shipment is classified as missing when no terminal scan or location update has
been recorded for more than 120 hours past the last confirmed location, with no
open documentation hold explaining the gap.

## Required actions

A missing-shipment classification requires an immediate control-tower escalation
and an internal investigation ticket; the customer should be told an
investigation is underway, with a committed follow-up time, not a guessed ETA.

## Compensation timing

No loss-of-cargo compensation may be offered until the shipment has been missing
for 30 days or the carrier confirms the loss in writing, whichever is sooner.
