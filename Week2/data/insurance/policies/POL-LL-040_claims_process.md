# POL-LL-040 Landlord Liability Claims Process

Policy type: landlord_liability
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Intake requirements

Every reported incident is logged for legal review before any settlement offer.
No admission of fault or payment may be communicated to the claimant directly by
the assistant or the policyholder.

## Escalation

Any incident referencing a minor, a fatality, or an attorney demand letter is
routed immediately to special_investigations regardless of stated severity.

## Timeline

Standard liability incidents receive a coverage determination within 15 business
days of receiving the completed incident report.
