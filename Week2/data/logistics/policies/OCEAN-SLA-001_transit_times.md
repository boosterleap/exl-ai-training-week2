# OCEAN-SLA-001 Ocean Freight Transit Times

Mode: ocean
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Contracted transit windows

Standard trans-Pacific routings (US West Coast to Southeast Asia) are contracted
at 12-16 calendar days port-to-port. Trans-Atlantic routings (Northern Europe to
US East Coast) are contracted at 8-11 calendar days port-to-port.

## Weather and routing exceptions

Reroutes for named storm advisories are not counted as SLA breaches provided the
carrier issues a revised ETA within 24 hours of the routing decision.

## Customer notification requirement

Any shipment expected to miss its contracted transit window by more than 24 hours
must be proactively notified to the consignee with a revised ETA before the
original contracted arrival date.
