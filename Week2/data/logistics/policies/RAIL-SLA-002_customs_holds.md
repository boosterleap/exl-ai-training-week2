# RAIL-SLA-002 Documentation and Customs Holds

Mode: rail
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Bill of lading mismatches

A container held for a bill of lading mismatch is not a carrier-caused delay and
is excluded from any delay compensation; the resolution owner is the shipper's
documentation team, not the carrier.

## Resolution timeline

Documentation holds should resolve within 5 business days of the mismatch being
identified. Holds exceeding this window must be escalated to the
documentation_review queue with a named owner.

## Customer communication

Customers may be told that a hold exists and the general reason category
(for example, "documentation mismatch"), but specific internal compliance notes
must not be shared externally.
