# POL-HOME-010 Water Damage Coverage

Policy type: homeowners
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Burst pipe coverage

Sudden and accidental discharge from plumbing systems, including burst pipes, is
covered up to USD 10,000 after the USD 1,000 deductible.

## Gradual seepage exclusion

Gradual seepage, maintenance neglect, and flood (defined separately) are excluded.
Flood coverage requires POL-HOME-FLOOD rider.

## Documentation

Claims must include photos, plumber invoice, and date-of-loss within 14 days.
