# POL-HEALTH-020 Prior Authorization

Policy type: group health
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Imaging services

MRI and CT scans require prior authorization when ordered outside an emergency visit.
Emergency imaging is covered without prior auth when documented within 48 hours.

## Specialty referrals

Specialist visits require PCP referral except for OB/GYN and behavioral health per
state mandate addendum POL-HEALTH-020-BH.

## Turnaround

Standard prior auth decisions publish within 72 hours; urgent requests within 24 hours.
