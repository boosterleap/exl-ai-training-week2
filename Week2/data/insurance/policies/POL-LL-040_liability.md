# POL-LL-040 Landlord Liability Coverage

Policy type: landlord_liability
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Covered incidents

The policy covers the named insured's legal liability for bodily injury or
property damage arising from ownership and maintenance of the rented premises,
up to USD 1,000,000 per occurrence, after the USD 1,000 deductible.

## Tenant negligence

Liability arising solely from a tenant's own negligence, and not from a defect or
maintenance failure attributable to the landlord, is excluded and should be
directed to the tenant's renters' policy.

## Reporting window

Incidents must be reported within 30 days of the date of loss, with a written
description of the incident and contact details for any injured party.
