# POL-AUTO-001 Comprehensive Auto Coverage

Policy type: comprehensive auto
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Covered perils

Comprehensive coverage pays for damage to the insured vehicle from non-collision events,
including theft, vandalism, fire, hail, and falling objects.

## Rental reimbursement

If the insured vehicle is stolen or declared a total loss, the policy reimburses rental
car expenses up to USD 40 per day for 30 days when the driver reports theft within
24 hours and cooperates with law enforcement.

## Out-of-state use

Vehicles garaged in the home state remain covered while temporarily used in other US
states for up to 90 consecutive days. Permanent relocation requires notifying the
carrier within 10 days.

## Deductible

Comprehensive claims are subject to a USD 500 deductible unless a waiver rider applies.
